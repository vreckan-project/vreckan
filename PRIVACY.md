# Privacy Policy for Vreckan

**Last Updated: 09-10-26**

## Introduction

Welcome to Vreckan. This privacy policy explains how the Vreckan web client ("the Client") handles your information. Vreckan is a client for a self-hosted platform designed to give you secure, remote access to applications. The fundamental principle of our design is user control and privacy.

**The Vreckan web client communicates ONLY with the server instance that you configure and control. We, the developers of Vreckan, do not have access to your server, your data, or any information transmitted between the Client and your server.**

## Information We Collect and Why

The Client stores a limited amount of data locally in your browser using its built-in storage. This data is necessary for the Client to function:

*   **Configuration Data:** To connect to your server, the Client stores your server address and your authentication and session details. This information is required to establish a secure, authenticated connection with your self-hosted Vreckan server.
*   **User Preferences:** The Client may store your saved launch preferences (e.g., automatically using a specific application for a certain file type) to improve your user experience.

When you use the Client, certain data is transmitted to YOUR server to perform actions you initiate:

*   **Authentication Information:** Your username and password (or single sign-on credentials) are sent to your server to verify your identity. The server then issues a session token, which the Client stores locally and presents on subsequent requests.
*   **Website Content:** When you open a link, image, or file in a remote application through the Client, the corresponding URL or content is sent to your server so it can be opened in the remote application.
*   **Files:** When you upload a file or intercept a download, the file's content is streamed directly and securely to your server.
*   **IP Address:** As with any internet connection, your IP address will be visible to your server when the Client communicates with it.

## How Your Information is Used

All collected information is used exclusively for the single purpose of the Client: to provide a secure and functional connection to your self-hosted Vreckan platform. We do not use this data for any other purpose, such as advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Data Storage and Security

All configuration data is stored locally in your browser using its built-in storage. All communication between the Client and your server's API is protected with End-to-End Encryption (E2EE), ensuring that the data in transit is secure.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to any third parties. The Client is designed to communicate solely with the server you specify. You are in complete control of where your data is sent.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
